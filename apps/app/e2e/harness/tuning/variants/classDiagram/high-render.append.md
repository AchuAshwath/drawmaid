## Class depth experiment

For a large or conceptually busy model, make the relationship kinds and the
most useful member distinctions easy to scan, using Mermaid constructs that
the source supports. A small model may remain plain. If classes have clear
conceptual groups, a restrained shared palette can help at High; use no more
than two or three shared fills and leave unrelated classes unstyled. Prefer a
simple valid class diagram over extra decoration or speculative structure.
