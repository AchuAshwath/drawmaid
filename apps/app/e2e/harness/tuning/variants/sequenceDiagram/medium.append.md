## Sequence Medium readability note

Retain the complete exchange and make source-supported context easy to scan:
show stated replies, label meaningful conditions or payloads, and use a real
`alt`/`else` or `loop` block when the input describes branching or repetition.
Use a concise `Note over` only when a phase needs context. Do not invent a
response, branch, participant, or note when the source does not provide one.
Keep every arrow labelled and keep message or note text plain and short so
punctuation cannot be mistaken for Mermaid syntax.
